About Algara

Design that honours light, texture, and everyday living.

Algara is a new design studio built on a simple belief: premium interiors should not demand a premium fortune. We work with homeowners, families, and businesses across India to create spaces that feel intentional — never overdone.

From the way light falls across a room to the grain of a handmade wooden piece, every detail is chosen with care. Our furniture line is limited and made by hand — a quiet, secondary offering for those who want something truly one of a kind.
